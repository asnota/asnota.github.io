“Redemption” is an emotional journey through regret, forgiveness, and the longing for a second chance. The song follows a voice confronting the consequences of a past decision, questioning whether what has been broken can still be restored. 



It is a reflection on guilt and the passage of time, but also on the possibility of change. The song does not offer a simple resolution. Instead, it ends with a question — “Is it?” — leaving open the possibility that even when everything appears to be lost, redemption may not yet be beyond reach.
